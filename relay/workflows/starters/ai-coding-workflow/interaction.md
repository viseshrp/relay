# Questions and human decisions in Relay

Use native form elicitation for questions, review discussions, AGREE,
RESOLVE, and the final go-ahead. Preserve every gate in the phase instructions.
Do not infer an answer from silence or finish with unresolved material
questions or pending human review.

If elicitation is unavailable, report the blocker instead of inventing
answers. The owner must complete the discussion before answering the next
workflow approval request.
